About The Venture

Building a Practical Mobility Model

Mobility & Hire-Purchase Services is being developed to address the challenges faced by commercial riders and entrepreneurs who need reliable motorcycles to generate income.

Many riders depend on motorcycles as an important source of income but may face significant barriers when trying to acquire their own asset.

Our model focuses on creating a structured hire-purchase pathway that combines access, accountability and responsible repayment.

Technology and data will also play an important role in improving record keeping, monitoring, decision-making and operational efficiency.
