About Us

Building Access to Better Mobility

Mobility & Hire-Purchase Services is an early-stage Nigerian mobility startup focused on improving access to motorcycles for people who depend on them for transportation and income.

Our Vision

We envision a system where hardworking Nigerians can access reliable motorcycles and gradually work toward ownership through structured and affordable payment arrangements.

Our long-term goal is to build a trusted mobility service that connects riders with accessible financing while supporting sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission

To make motorcycle access and ownership more achievable for commercial riders and small business owners through responsible hire-purchase solutions.

The Challenge

A Capital Barrier to Earning

For many riders, owning a motorcycle can be an important step toward earning a sustainable income. However, purchasing one outright can be difficult.

01

High Upfront Cost

The full cost of purchasing a motorcycle can be difficult for many riders and aspiring entrepreneurs to raise at once.

02

Limited Access to Capital

People with the ability to work and generate income may still lack access to suitable financing options.

03

Missed Economic Opportunities

Without access to mobility assets, potential riders can miss opportunities to earn income and support their families.

Our Proposed Solution

Access Today. Work Toward Ownership.

Our proposed model will allow eligible riders to access motorcycles through structured hire-purchase arrangements.

Affordable Access

Reduce the need for riders to provide the entire motorcycle purchase price upfront.

Structured Payments

Riders will make agreed regular payments under transparent hire-purchase arrangements.

Path to Ownership

The model is designed to provide a clear pathway toward eventual motorcycle ownership.

Our Model

How It Is Expected to Work

The proposed process is designed to be simple, transparent and manageable.

1

Apply

Eligible riders provide their details and mobility requirements.

2

Assessment

Applications will be reviewed against established eligibility and affordability criteria.

3

Access

Approved riders will receive access to a motorcycle under agreed terms.

4

Pay & Own

Riders make regular payments while working toward ownership according to the agreement.

Expected Impact

More Than Transportation

Our proposed business aims to create economic opportunities alongside improved access to mobility.

Income Opportunities

Help riders access motorcycles that can support income-generating activities.

Youth Empowerment

Create opportunities for young Nigerians to build livelihoods through mobility-based work.

Asset Ownership

Create a pathway for eligible riders to work toward owning productive assets.
