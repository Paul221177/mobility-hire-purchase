About Us

Mobility & Hire-Purchase Services is a Nigerian mobility startup providing affordable motorcycle hire-purchase solutions to commercial riders and small business owners.

The Problem We Solve

Many Nigerians depend on motorcycles for transportation and income but cannot afford the full purchase price upfront. Our solution makes reliable motorcycles more accessible through structured payment arrangements.

Our Services

Motorcycle Hire-Purchase

Access motorcycles through affordable and structured payment arrangements.

Rider Mobility

Helping commercial riders obtain reliable motorcycles for income-generating activities.

Path to Ownership

Riders make regular payments while working toward eventual ownership of their motorcycle.

How It Works

1. Apply

Eligible riders provide their information and mobility needs.

2. Get a Motorcycle

Approved riders receive access to a motorcycle under agreed terms.

3. Pay & Own

Regular payments are made according to the agreed plan.
